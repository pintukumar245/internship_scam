Recognize Internship Scam Traps

1. Demands for Money

No legitimate company will ask for **Registration Fee**, **Training Charges**, or **Security Deposit** to offer an internship. If they ask for money, it's 100% a scam.

2. Fraud Certificates

Some portals sell fake internship certificates for simple copy-paste work. Such certificates have zero value in any job interview.

3. Gmail or WhatsApp Hiring

If HR contacts you from a free email account (like info.xyz@gmail.com) without a corporate domain, or strictly chats on WhatsApp, definitely do your research.
